## 2.1.0 — 2026-09-28

### Added

- Pre-test briefing using upcoming Google Calendar tests, recent performance, weak areas, and saved mistakes.
- User-scoped adaptive study schedules through the Claude MCP connector.
- Screenshot and pasted-result ingestion, mistake resurfacing, automatic tags, and cross-test pattern summaries.
- Compact, expandable mistake and AI-analysis lists.

### Changed

- Added theme-specific accent colors and reorganized the top action bar.
- Updated the Advanced settings roadmap to show completed and partial stages accurately.
